Page Connect

Open any page at an exact time, or watch a store's product page and add the item to your cart when it's available.

The datetime is interpreted as wall-clock time in the selected timezone (default Pacific).

Works on Pokémon Center, Target, Walmart, Sam's Club, Best Buy, Costco, GameStop and Amazon. Sign in to the store first. You check out and pay yourself, and you complete any sign-in or human check the store shows.

Alerts when the page opens, when the site asks you to sign in or complete a human check, when you reach the product page, and when your cart is ready. Sent through bretttomita.com.

Idle. Configure URL and time, then press Start.
